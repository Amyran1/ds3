F08. Action survival / hazard

pooled AR=9.87%; hazard@lag0=39.18%; hazard@lag30=2.05%; streaks=115,327; one-and-done=202,787 [full n=129,190,540 wall=47s]

Wave 3IMPORTANTfeature-eng

Question

After a user signs a petition, what's the distribution of 'sends until next action'? Does action probability spike, decay, or persist? Are there 'streak users' vs 'one-and-done'?

In plain English

After someone watches a movie they loved, are they MORE likely to watch another in the next week — primed and energized — or LESS likely — satisfied and saturated? Different people behave differently. Some go on streaks. Some are one-and-done.

The 'hazard curve' answers: on the send right after signing, what's the action rate? On send #5 after? Send #30? If the curve spikes right after a signature vs the 9.9% baseline, users are primed — send more, fast. If it drops, they're satisfied — give them a rest.

Most production models miss this temporal pattern entirely. F08 measures it and creates cheap features like `is_in_action_streak`, `sends_since_last_action`. These may outperform the fancy embedding feature on simpler grounds.

Method

sample = user_emails.sample_percentage(None, seed=42)  # full

# Sort by (user_id, date_sent); compute sends_since_last_action
# lag=0 on actioned row; resets to 0 on next send after action
df = sample.sort(["user_id", "date_sent"])
df = df.with_columns(
    pl.col("actioned").cast(pl.Int32).shift(1, fill_value=0).over("user_id").alias("_prev_actioned"),
).with_columns(
    pl.col("_prev_actioned").cum_sum().over("user_id").alias("_epoch"),
).with_columns(
    pl.int_range(pl.len(), dtype=pl.Int64).over(["user_id", "_epoch"]).alias("sends_since_last_action"),
)

# Hazard curve: mean(actioned) per lag k in [0, 60]
hazard = (
    df.filter(pl.col("sends_since_last_action") <= 60)
    .group_by("sends_since_last_action")
    .agg(pl.col("actioned").mean().alias("hazard"), pl.len().alias("n"))
    .sort("sends_since_last_action")
)

Impact on harness / features

**Hazard SPIKES at lag 0**: P(action | next send after action) = 39.2% vs pooled 9.9% (4.0× lift). Users are primed immediately after acting. Feature `sends_since_last_action` is high-value; low lag values are strong positive signal.

Hazard decays quickly to baseline by lag 5 (9.6%), lag 30 (2.0%). Only the immediate post-action window matters.

**Recommended features**: `sends_since_last_action`, `is_in_action_streak` (boolean flag for users currently in a ≥3-send streak), `action_in_last_5_sends` (boolean). These are cheap to compute per-send from the user_emails entity.

**Streak users** (115,327) tolerate lower per-email match quality — useful for exploration sends. **One-and-done users** (202,787) are the target for re-engagement.
